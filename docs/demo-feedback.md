# Demo feedback notes

Informal reactions collected by the project creator during the hackathon on **25 October 2026**. These are creator-reported recollections of conversations with the Mubit team — not official endorsements, judge scores, or award decisions.

## What we heard

- The **CTO** described the concept as fun and cool.
- The **CEO** liked the idea. After being shown the demo video and the trailer, he enjoyed both.
- Technical feedback highlighted the **choice graph** and its **constraints** — preferences interpreted through conversation, then validated and built by the server rather than generated freely.
- Feedback on the use of **Mubit** for the recurring story-decision task was positive.

## Context

The team saw the playable loop described in the [demo guide](demo.md): a player answers Mara's leads in their own words, the director interprets that into a chapter decision, and the Paper server builds the chosen realm with evidence-gated progression through the [quest graph](quest-graph.md).

These notes record encouragement during development. They carry no marks and predict no outcome; the submission stands on the [recorded evidence](submission-evidence.md) and the code in this repository.
